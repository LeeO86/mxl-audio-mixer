# mxl-audio-mixer 1.0.0 — the maintainers' checklist

What CI cannot test: real nodes, real networks, real browsers and the platform's
services. Each item: what to run, what passes. The results go into the release
notes ("Tested", on hardware) before the tag.

Image: the `nightly-dev` image of the release commit (or a local build); the
platform settings as in `docs/release-notes/1.0.0.md` ("What the platform must
set").

## Nodes and MXL

- [ ] **Block clock** on a platform node (SCHED_FIFO 49, an exclusive CPU):
  `tests/hw/m0-block-clock/run.sh 60`. Pass: 0 deadline misses, the largest
  jitter under 2 ms, 0 read or write errors, 0 mismatched samples.
- [ ] **§16 load for an hour** on a platform node (release build, the mixer's
  pod settings): `SECONDS=3600 tests/perf/section16.sh`. Pass: `dsp_load_ratio`
  under 0.5, 0 deadline misses (§16). Note the mean block, the slowest bucket,
  and `nr_throttled` in the pod's `cpu.stat`.
- [ ] **Mirrored outputs** across two nodes with mxl-fabrics-agent:
  `tests/hw/m2-mirrored-output/run.sh source` (node A) and `… check …` (node B).
  Pass: full sample rate on node B, 0 gaps, sample error under 1e-5, 0 missing
  samples at a downstream mixer.
- [ ] **Inputs from the platform's functions** (mxl-decklink, the ST 2110
  gateway, mxl-test-player): routed by IS-05 from the platform's controller,
  `running`, an automatic delay that settles, no missing samples over 10 min
  (`/api/v1/inputs`).
- [ ] **Outputs to the platform's functions**: a published bus and a published
  monitor received by mxl-decklink or the ST 2110 gateway, on physical speakers.

## NMOS

- [ ] **AMWA on a platform node**: `ci/amwa.sh off target/release/mxl-audio-mixer`
  there (it runs its own nmos-cpp registry). Pass: no failures, no warnings, the
  counts as in CI (record them).
- [ ] **The platform's registry**: with `NMOS_REGISTRY_ADDRESS`/`PORT` set, the
  node, device, receivers and senders are registered and listed by its Query API
  (`/readyz` 200), and gone after SIGTERM.
- [ ] **The platform's controller** sees the node, its receivers (`Input <n>`) and
  senders (published buses, `Monitor <name>`), and connects them.

## Browser monitoring and talkback

- [ ] **Monitoring** through the platform's MediaMTX (`PREVIEW_*` set): Listen on
  a monitor card in a browser on the operators' network; the stream starts within
  a few seconds and stops 10 s after closing. The latency by ear or with the
  `second-pulse` oscillator (CI: 79–99 ms median in headless Chrome).
- [ ] **Monitoring without the shared MediaMTX** (built-in, ICE UDP 8199 by
  hostPort): the same.
- [ ] **Browser talkback** on the HTTPS UI: "This browser's microphone", Mic on,
  talk to an IFB destination; heard on the IFB output; interrupt cuts its
  programme; the control room is not dimmed by a browser mic.
- [ ] **Talkback widget** in the production designer (`allow="microphone"` on
  the frame): talk from it.
- [ ] **Personal monitor** ("My headphones") with its own PFL, while the control
  room's monitors keep theirs.

## Control room

- [ ] **Speakers**: a 5.1 monitor published to physical speakers; level, dim,
  cut, the checks, the SPL preset with a calibration; bass management on and off
  (the sub carries the lows).
- [ ] **Talkback from an MXL mic**: a latched and a held talk to the studio and
  an IFB; the control room dims; priority between two mics on an IFB.
- [ ] **Listen-back** of a destination's return.

## Tally and widgets

- [ ] **Tally** from the platform's tally calculator (TSL UMD 5.0 over UDP and
  TCP): the strip lamps follow; audio-follows-video opens and closes with the
  fades.
- [ ] **Widgets** in the production designer (`WIDGET_FRAME_ANCESTORS` set):
  `strip`, `fader-bank` (with a VCA in a page), `loudness`, `monitor`,
  `talkback`, `vca` framed and working; a frame from another origin refused.

## VCAs, groups, N-1 (milestone 6)

- [ ] **VCAs**: nested VCAs on the console, spill, a VCA PFL; a fader at +15 dB
  under VCAs stays at +15 dB.
- [ ] **Mute groups** with a mute-safe strip (also under a muted VCA).
- [ ] **N-1** for a remote guest from the programme: the guest hears everything
  but themselves; it follows the programme's faders.
- [ ] **Group refused with a mix-minus** (maintainer's answer 2 of M6b): make an
  N-1 of the programme for a strip, then try to group that strip from the
  Operations panel. Pass: refused with a message naming the N-1; after deleting
  the N-1 the group works and the programme sounds the same.
- [ ] **Link and unlink** a stereo source: the sound is the same before, linked
  and after.

## Life cycle

- [ ] **Restart and upgrade**: the pod restarted and replaced by the release
  image: the show, the connections and the NMOS ids survive; SIGTERM exits 143
  within `SHUTDOWN_TIMEOUT_S`, the node is deregistered.
