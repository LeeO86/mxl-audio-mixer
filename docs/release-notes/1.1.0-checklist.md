# mxl-audio-mixer 1.1.0: the maintainers' checklist (milestone 7)

**Breaking default.** `WEB_PORT` is 8190 and `NMOS_PORT` is 3342 (events
WebSocket 3343). 1.0.0 used 8160 and 3312. A deployment that did not set them
moves. Set the old values to stay put. See `docs/release-notes/1.1.0.md`.

Voice detection, auto-level, the auto-mixer and ducking need **recordings of
productions**: CI only has synthetic speech (espeak-ng) and tones. Each item:
what to run, what passes. The results go into the release notes ("Tested")
before the tag.

## Recordings to bring

WAV files (16-bit PCM or 32-bit float, any sample rate, any channel count;
`mxl-mixer-tone --wav` plays the mean of the channels on every channel, so a
5.1 bed is six mono tracks, one writer each, patched into a 5.1 strip):
- **a panel or talk show as isolated microphone tracks** (multitrack, all the
  same length and start), with crosstalk between the microphones as recorded;
- **a commentary track with its programme bed** (crowd, ambience), also as two
  tracks; best a bed with an interview in it, and one in 5.1;
- **a presenter over music** (the presenter's microphone and the music as
  tracks);
- a few minutes of **music only** (jingles, beds, songs with singing) and of
  **room tone only**.

Playing them into the mixer: one `mxl-mixer-tone` per track, started together,
each on its own input (`--wav track.wav`). Sample i of every writer plays
position i modulo the length, so tracks of the same length stay aligned:

```sh
for t in host guest1 guest2; do
  mxl-mixer-tone --domain /Volumes/mxl/tracks --flow "$(uuidgen)" --channels 1 --wav "$t.wav" &
done
```

## Voice detection

- [ ] **Offline, per track:** `VAD_WAV=host.wav cargo test --release --lib evaluate -- --ignored --nocapture`
  prints one character per 10 ms (`#` speech), for Silero (the default) and the
  feature detector (`VAD_DETECTOR=silero` or `features` for one). Listen along
  at the marked places. Pass for Silero: phrases marked from their first
  syllable on (onsets within about 100 ms); pauses longer than the hold
  (0.8 s) unmarked; room tone, tones, jingles and instrumental music unmarked.
  Note how much music with singing is marked, and where the two detectors
  differ.
- [ ] **The gate against crosstalk:** play the panel tracks, put voice
  detection on every microphone (Auto panel). For each microphone read its
  talker's `momentary_lufs` (`GET /api/v1/vad`) and set `gate_lufs` 20–30 dB
  below it (default −32 LUFS, to be tried). Pass: while only another
  microphone's talker speaks, the microphone shows `gated` and no speech; its
  own talker is detected. Note the gate values that work, and whether −32 LUFS
  should stay the default.

## Auto-mix

- [ ] **Panel, gain sharing:** an auto-mix of the panel microphones
  (last-mic-hold on), the programme on speakers. Pass, by ear: the talker's
  microphone open, the others down without audible noise swells; no pumping in
  pauses between sentences; two talkers at once both clear; with nobody
  talking the room stays as it was. Note the gains (`GET
  /api/v1/automixes/<id>`, `gains_db`) at a few places.
- [ ] **Weights and bypass:** a weight of −6 dB on a loud guest, a bypassed
  presenter (always 0 dB). Pass: as described in the README, by ear and in
  `gains_db`.
- [ ] **VAD weighting** on the panel with crosstalk. Note whether it helps
  (fewer wrong microphones open) or not.

## Ducking

- [ ] **Music under the presenter:** a duck with the presenter as key and the
  music as target (depth −12 dB, attack 50 ms, hold 800 ms, release
  1000 ms). Pass, by ear: the music down at the presenter's first syllable,
  staying down through the pauses between sentences (no swell at sentence
  rate), back after the presenter stops. Note values that sound better.
- [ ] **Commentary over the bed:** the commentary as key, the bed as target.
  Pass: as above; the bed's own speech (an interview in the bed) does not duck
  it (its own key is not the commentary).
- [ ] **Commentary over the bed, ratio mode** (`mode: "ratio"`, Com-IS-Assist's
  control, its defaults: 5 LU, over-voice 13 LU, at most 24 dB, speed 1). Pass,
  by ear: the commentary stays on top of a loud and of a quiet bed alike (the
  duck's `control.ratio_lu` near 5 LU while the commentator talks); a crowd
  surge does not bury him (the fast stage); the bed does not swell in the
  pauses between his sentences; an interview in the bed while he is silent
  comes up quickly (`control.state` `interview`); both at once
  (`overvoice`) keep him intelligible. Note values (ratio, speed) that sound
  better, and compare with Com-IS-Assist's plugin on the same tracks if at hand.
- [ ] **A 5.1 bed, ratio mode,** `channel_depth` `{"lfe": 0, "surround": 0.5}`.
  Pass: the front and centre duck, the surrounds half as much in dB, the LFE
  not at all; the atmosphere stays.

## Auto-level

- [ ] **A quiet and a loud guest** (the panel tracks, one 10 dB down by its
  trim), each auto-levelled with the defaults (−23 LUFS, +2 LU for speech,
  ±12 dB, 1 s / 3 s, freeze below −50 LUFS). Pass: both end up at about the
  same loudness (the programme's loudness meter, short-term around −21 LUFS
  while they talk); no audible pumping within sentences; the gain holds
  (`state.frozen`) in pauses and room tone instead of rising; music on an
  auto-levelled signal goes to −23 LUFS. Note values that sound better.
- [ ] **With the auto-mix:** the panel auto-mixed and auto-levelled. Pass: the
  sharing follows the levelled microphones (a quiet guest gets his share).

## Snapshots and the chain

- [ ] **Moderation, then panel:** a snapshot with the presenter's duck and no
  auto-mix, another with the panel auto-mix and no duck; recall them in turn
  during playback. Pass: no clicks, the auto-mix sharing and the duck as in
  each snapshot; auto-levels as in each snapshot (a recall-safe one stays).
- [ ] **The auto gain in the chain:** move a member's `auto` node (Processing
  tab, "Auto gain") before its compressor. Pass: the compressor works on the
  auto-mixed level; without the node the gain acts before the fader, and a
  pre-fader aux carries it.

## Live and load

- [ ] **Live microphones** in a studio on a platform node: two or three
  microphones, an auto-mix and a duck of a music bed. Pass: the voice dots
  follow the talkers in the UI, nothing audible in the programme that the
  recordings did not show.
- [ ] **Load:** 64 analysed signals (voice detection on 64 strips, Silero) and 8
  auto-mixes on a platform node. Note `process_cpu_seconds_total` per second
  (about half a core is expected for 64 Silero detectors) and the control
  side's latency (the voice dots following); the DSP thread's
  `dsp_load_ratio` must stay under 0.5 (§16); `vad_drops_total` stays 0.
