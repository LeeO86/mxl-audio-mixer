# mxl-audio-mixer 1.4.3 — release notes

A small fix on top of 1.4.2. Released 2026-10-24.

## For the platform

- No setting, metric, API, port or show-file change. `MIXER_READ_OFFSET` stays 2. Rollback to 1.4.2 or 1.4.1 is clean.
- Everything of 1.4.2 holds (`MIXER_STATE_DIR/input-delays.json`, the start-up phase).

## Change (PR #49)

- A flow that goes away and comes back on the same route (a writer restart) keeps the input's last lag. Its new reader is counted by the stall rule (0.5 s at real-time pace, or back within a block of the old lag), not by the 0.1 s start span of D243. Only a route to another flow forgets the last lag. Before, a re-created flow whose writer started behind and caught up slowly could raise that input's delay for about a minute. Found in the review of 1.4.2.

## Tested

CI on the merge commit: lint, unit tests, image, integration (libmxl, nmos-cpp registry, AMWA) including `tests/integration/inputs.sh`.
